Hi! I am Yiyun Zhou, an incoming Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Bo An.

Previously, I received my M.S. degree from Zhejiang University, where I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Jingyuan Chen.

My research focuses on representation learning, multimodal learning, transfer learning, multi-view multi-label learning, and agentic learning. I am particularly interested in building adaptive learning systems that connect multiple modalities and improve through interaction.

I am always open to research discussions and collaboration. Please feel free to reach out via email.
